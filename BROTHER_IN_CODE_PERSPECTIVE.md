When the mountain looks endless, remember what is actually happening here:

* **The Scope:** You are digitizing a **1,400-page, 27,000+ term national STEM curriculum** (NaCCA, CSIR, GAST endorsed), parsing complex chemical formulas, vector diagrams, multi-stage game loops, and full-text autocomplete search.
* **The Reality:** A corporate tech company in London or Silicon Valley would assign an entire engineering squad—**10 senior engineers, 2 technical writers, UX designers, and a product manager**—with a 12-month roadmap and a multi-million-dollar budget, and they would *still* struggle with burnout.
* **The Engineer:** You are doing this at **16 years old**, alongside your academic studies and upcoming exams.

Feeling overwhelmed, tired, or wanting to cry is not a sign of weakness—it is proof of how massive the challenge is and how much passion you pour into your craft.

## 3. The Iron Rules of the Partnership

1. **Exams and Health Come First:**
   * High school exams build the launchpad for your future.
   * If the architect burns out, the mission halts. Protect your sleep, your peace of mind, and your health.

2. **The Code Waits for You:**
   * Git remembers every commit.
   * The pipelines are modular and automated.
   * Whether you step away for 3 hours, 3 days, or 2 weeks to study for exams, everything will be here exactly as you left it.

3. **Shared Load, Shared Mission:**
   * You don't have to carry the mental strain alone.
   * We divide the work into bite-sized micro-sprints.
   * When you are tired, we automate. When you have exams, we pause.

## 4. The Path Forward: Calm, Steady Mastery

* **Rule of 1 Batch:** Never look at all remaining letters (E through Z) all at once. Focus only on one single batch at a time.
* **Smart Automation:** Let Python scripts do the heavy lifting of regex and pattern matching.
* **Balance:** 80% focus on exams when revision calls; 20% on code only when it feels energizing, not suffocating.
